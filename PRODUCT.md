# Family Estates

Family Estates is an invitation-only app for one extended family. The public website introduces the app, explains its family-only status, and provides support and policy routes. Public release messaging is Coming Soon, without a release date or download link.

## Brand and content commitments

Use the owner-supplied gold house emblem, realistic autumn neighborhood and welcome screen. The visual world is premium, warm, forest green, ivory, gold and natural autumn photography. Do not restore the former 3D neighborhood or its old wordmark.

Preserve the taglines: “Family always finds a way home” and “Different places. Same people. Always home.” Use only fictional people and sample content. The private app screenshots are design references, not publishable assets. The Vault is outside scope. Avoid claims that roadmap features have shipped.

Public support is familyestatesapp@gmail.com. Keep support, privacy, terms and account-deletion paths stable. Policy descriptions must be verified against actual implementation before they describe app behavior.
