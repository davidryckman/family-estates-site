Privacy Policy

How Family Estates handles information, in the app and on this website.

Scope & operator

This policy covers the Family Estates iPhone app and this public website, operated by PrinceRedBeard. Effective November 20, 2026. Contact: familyestatesapp@gmail.com.

Family Estates is a private, invitation-only app for one extended family. We do not show ads, sell personal information, share it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites. The app uses no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Information we collect

  • Account: your Sign in with Apple identifier, and the name and email Apple shares with the app (which may be a private relay address).
  • Family profile and membership: your display name, photo, household, family relationships and role, plus a birthday, anniversaries, email, phone and home address if you choose to add them. A member needs either a birthday on their profile or an administrator’s confirmation that they are 13 or older before they can have their own sign-in.
  • What you share: memories, photos, videos, voice recordings, messages, comments, reactions, polls, calendar events and RSVPs, recipes, game entries and scores, family-tree records with their notes and media, time capsules and family-interview answers. Voice recordings may be transcribed on your device; a transcript leaves your device only if it is part of something you share.
  • Calendar events you import: if you link Apple Calendar, only the calendars you choose are copied into your household’s calendar in the app.
  • Location for weather: the city and region your household enters, used to show weather and severe-weather check-ins. Your phone’s approximate location is used on the device only to match the Estate’s daylight; it is not sent to us.
  • Notifications: a device token and installation identifier so Apple can deliver alerts you allow.
  • Age eligibility: only family members aged 13 or older can have their own sign-in. We record whether a profile is eligible, based on its birthday or, where there is none, an administrator’s confirmation with who confirmed it and when. We do not ask for or keep proof of age.
  • Safety: reports you send and the members you block.
  • Service logs: our hosting provider keeps short-lived request logs, such as IP address and time, to operate and secure the service.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, health, financial or advertising data.

How it is used & who can see it

Information is used only to run the app: sign-in, showing the right content to the right family members, notifications, weather, games and keeping the Estate safe.

  • Audience: content is visible to the audience you choose: the whole Estate, your household, selected members, or only you. Direct and group messages are visible only to their participants.
  • Moderators: Estate owners and stewards can review reports, including a short excerpt of reported content that the reporter could already see, and can remove reported comments or messages.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in sessions, hosted in the United States) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications, WeatherKit) process data on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Sports schedules: the app retrieves public St. Louis Blues schedules and scores from the NHL’s public data service. No account information is sent, but as with any internet request the service can see the device’s IP address.

Private does not mean end-to-end encrypted. Data is encrypted in transit and stored with access limited by database rules. Private photos and recordings are delivered through short-lived authorized links.

Device permissions

The app asks only when a feature needs it. Each is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.

  • Camera and microphone: to take photos and videos and record voice messages and interview answers.
  • Photos: to choose pictures to share and to save a memory’s media to your library.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe recordings on your device.
  • Calendars: to import the Apple calendars you pick.
  • Location (while using the app): to match the Estate’s daylight on your device.
  • Notifications: to alert you to messages, games and family events.

Children & teens

Family Estates is not offered to children under 13 as users. Family administrators do not invite children under 13, and the service does not let a child under 13 claim an account. A family may include a child’s name, household, relationships, birthday, game results, and photos or stories about the child in profiles, memories and the family tree. A parent or guardian can ask us to correct or delete that information at any time at familyestatesapp@gmail.com.

Members aged 13 to 17 sign in with their own Apple account and have the same privacy controls as adults. Parents do not automatically see a teen’s private messages.

Reporting & blocking

You can report content or a member from the app and follow the status of your reports under Settings → My Reports. Reports go only to Estate moderators, and the reported member is not told who reported them. You can block a member from the content’s menu. Blocking hides their content from you and stops private messages between you, and they are not notified. You can unblock under Settings → Blocked Members.

Keeping & deleting information

We keep your information while you are a member, unless you delete it sooner. You can delete your account inside the app; the steps and exactly what is removed or kept are on the Account deletion page.

  • Shared items: events, polls and conversations other members took part in remain with their contributions; yours are removed. Game and Cup results remain labeled “Former member” unless you choose to keep your name. “Former member” is not a promise of anonymity to people who know the family.
  • Time capsules you sealed for family members stay and open on the day you chose, with your name on them. Delete one before deleting your account to take it back.
  • Reports: kept while the Estate exists; when a reporter or reported member deletes their account, their identity is removed from the report.
  • Backups: database backups are kept for 7 days, so deleted information is gone from backups within 7 days. Photos, videos and recordings are removed when deleted.
  • Deleting the app or signing out does not delete your account.

Your choices

Edit or delete your content in the app, turn notifications off in iOS Settings, and delete your account in Settings. To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or help with deletion, email familyestatesapp@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm the request from the Apple account linked to the Estate. Never send passwords or verification codes.

This website

The website has no login, account registration or forms. Its code uses no cookies, analytics, local storage, tracking scripts or external fonts, and its pages work without JavaScript. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes technical request information, including IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. An email link opens your own email app, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Changes

We will update this page and tell members in the app before material changes take effect.
