Scope & operator

This notice covers the public Family Estates information website, operated by PrinceRedBeard. Contact: familyestatesapp@gmail.com.

This notice does not describe data handling inside the Family Estates app and must not be used as the app’s privacy policy.

What the website does

The website has no login or account-registration form and does not display private app account data. People and content in public examples are fictional.

The website code uses no cookies, analytics, local storage, tracking scripts, embedded forms, or external fonts. Its pages work without JavaScript.

Hosting

The live website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, including IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement.

Email & privacy questions

An email link opens your chosen email application. No message is sent unless you choose to send it. If you email support, the contact details and message you send are provided to the support inbox.

Include only what is needed to explain your question. For questions about support correspondence or a privacy request, contact familyestatesapp@gmail.com.

This website notice does not promise a retention period or deletion timeline for app information or support messages.
