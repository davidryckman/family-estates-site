Contact support

Email familyestatesapp@gmail.com for help with Family Estates or a question about this website.

For a technical problem, include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a short description of what happened.

Membership & availability

Family Estates is an invitation-only space for our family. Visiting this website does not create an app account or grant Estate membership.

The app is coming soon. This website does not offer public registration or a public app download. Contact support if you need help with an invitation or installation.

Privacy & account questions

For a privacy concern or an account-deletion question, contact familyestatesapp@gmail.com. This website has no sign-in or account-management controls.

Read Website Privacy and Account Deletion for the scope of the information currently published here.

Share only what is needed

Do not send passwords, verification codes, invitation tokens, identity documents, health information, or private family photographs and messages. If a screenshot helps explain an issue, remove identifying and sensitive details first.
