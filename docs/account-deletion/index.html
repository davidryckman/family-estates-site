Ask for account guidance

For an account-deletion question, email familyestatesapp@gmail.com. This public website has no login or account-deletion controls.

What this page confirms

A verified end-to-end app account-deletion procedure is not yet published here. This page provides a contact route for questions. It is not a functioning account-deletion service.

No completion time, universal-erasure claim, or retention exception is promised here. The handling of shared content, media, backups, and young members requires verified app instructions.

Before taking action

Deleting an app from your device or signing out does not itself delete an account or server-stored information.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, invitation tokens, identity documents, or private family media in an initial support request.
