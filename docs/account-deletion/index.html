Account Deletion

Delete your Family Estates account and what you have shared.

Delete in the appWhat is deletedWhat is keptNeed help?

Delete in the app

  1. Open Family Estates and go to Rooms.
  2. Tap the gear to open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete My Account and read what will happen.
  4. Optionally turn on Keep my family-tree notes or Keep my name on game results. Both are off unless you turn them on.
  5. Tap Delete My Account and confirm with Apple. Apple also disconnects the app from your Apple ID.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Deleting the app from your phone or signing out does not delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, profile photo, birthday, age-eligibility record, contact details and device notification settings.
  • Your memories, messages, comments, replies, votes, RSVPs and drafts.
  • Photos, videos and recordings you uploaded, including copies and transcripts.
  • Your access to every household and the Estate.

Database backups are kept for 7 days, so deleted information is gone from backups within 7 days.

What is kept

  • Events, polls and conversations that other family members joined stay, with everyone else’s contributions. Yours are removed.
  • Game and Cup results stay for the family’s history, shown as “Former member”, unless you chose to keep your name. That label alone does not make someone anonymous to people who know the family.
  • Time capsules you sealed for family members, with your name on them. They still open on the day you chose. To take one back, delete it in Archives → Time Capsules first.
  • A bare record that an account existed and was removed, kept for safety. It holds no name, photo or contact details.
  • Family-tree notes and suggestions, only if you chose to keep them.

Need help?

If you cannot open the app, or want a parent or guardian to ask us to remove a child’s information, email familyestatesapp@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm the request from the Apple account linked to the Estate. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, invitation tokens, identity documents or private family media in an initial request.
